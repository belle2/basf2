🔬 Belle II IR Region Geometry - Interactive Viewer

How to Use

  • Hover over any component to see its details in a tooltip:
    • Component name (e.g., A1spc1)
    • Description (e.g., "Right HER Beam pipe space 1")
    • Material (e.g., Vacuum, Steel, Tungsten)
    • Mother volume (the volume that contains this component)
    • Daughter volumes (components inside this volume)
  • Get close to a vertex (corner of a volume) to see:
    • Plane number and whether it's an inner or outer radius
    • Exact Z and R coordinates in cm
  • Click on a component to activate it:
    • Component gets red outline
    • Information panel appears on the right showing all details
    • Complete table of planes with Z, R_inner, R_outer coordinates
    • Click again or use × button to close
  • Click on a material in the legend (right side):
    • All components of that material get red outlines
    • No info panel shown - useful for quick overview
    • Click again to deselect
  • Click on a volume in the list (right side):
    • All 106 volumes listed alphabetically in multiple columns
    • Click any name to activate with full details
    • Quick navigation alternative to clicking on the diagram
  • Components highlight when you hover over them
  • Scroll to zoom, drag to pan (browser dependent)

About the Geometry

This interactive visualization shows a radial cross-section (R-Z view) of the Belle II IR Region geometry. The IR Region contains the VTX, Beampipe, and shielding components.

  • Z axis (horizontal): Longitudinal position along the beam line (cm)
  • R axis (vertical): Radial distance from the beam axis (cm)
  • Right side (positive Z): Forward direction (HER and LER beam pipes, A/B/C components)
  • Left side (negative Z): Backward direction (D/E/F components)

Generated from: various XML files in ir/data and vtx/data
Total volumes: 106
Z range: -792 to +876 cm
R range: ±82.5 cm
Features: Click activation, material filtering, volume list navigation, volume hierarchy, vertex detection, plane table display
